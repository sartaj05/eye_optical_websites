FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about our services. Can't find the answer? Just WhatsApp us!

Do you offer free eye tests?
Yes! We offer a basic free eye checkup for all walk-in customers. A detailed comprehensive eye exam (including eye pressure test and fundus check) is available at ₹199 only.
What brands of frames do you carry?
We stock a wide range of brands including Rayban, Fastrack, Titan Eye+, Oakley, Vogue, Carrera, John Jacobs, and many local affordable brands starting from ₹299. We have 500+ frame styles in stock.
How long does it take to get glasses made?
Standard single-vision lenses are ready in 1–2 hours. Bifocal and progressive lenses may take 24–48 hours. We'll let you know the exact time after checking your prescription.
Do you accept UPI / online payment?
Yes! We accept cash, UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), and all major debit/credit cards. No extra charges for digital payments.
Can I get lenses for my old frame?
Absolutely! Bring your old frame and we'll fit new prescription lenses in it. We can also repair or adjust your old frame if needed — usually for free or at minimal cost.
Is eye testing available for children?
Yes! We specialize in kids vision screening from age 3 onwards. Our friendly staff makes the experience comfortable for children. School vision certificates are also provided for free.
What are your shop timings?
We are open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays. For urgent queries on Sundays, you can WhatsApp us at +91 90447 90354 and we'll respond as soon as possible.
Do you offer home delivery?
Currently we do not offer home delivery, but you can place your order via WhatsApp and arrange for someone to pick it up. We're exploring home delivery options for Pratapgarh city soon!
